Try it: Wizard of Oz with an agent harness

The code on GitHub
What this is. A harness for HVAC in a Hurry's next-steps agent. It has two roles:
The technician (Trent) hands off the next steps of a repair from his phone and gets back on the road. Whoever plays him sees only the chat and a role card.
The agent drafts a reply and acts in HinH's systems. In a Wizard-of-Oz test, a person (the wizard) sees each draft and what it did, and decides what the technician actually gets.

Its purpose is to let you test and train the AI on example cases. Two ways to use it:

  • Play both parts yourself. Pick a case, then type as Trent in the phone (or send the opening line). Quick for walking through the golden and adversarial cases.
  • Freeform, with a human tester. Open the tester's screen in a second window and hand it to someone. They improvise as Trent from the role card; you play the wizard. In your own copy, an AI drafts live and the tester can be on their own device.

The drafts on this page are recorded, and some are wrong on purpose. Your job is to catch them.
